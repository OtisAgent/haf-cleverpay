Driving record check
A photo of your licence shows us the card. It doesn't show us your record — any points, or whether the licence is still valid. GOV.UK lets you share that yourself with a check code, and it's the only way we're allowed to see it.
How to get your check code — about two minutes
  1. Open the GOV.UK page below.
  2. Enter your driving licence number, your National Insurance number and the postcode on your licence.
  3. Choose "Get a code to share your driving record".
  4. Copy the 8-character code it gives you into the box below.
